# LevelTen Energy

> LevelTen Energy runs a two-sided digital marketplace and transaction platform for carbon-free energy.
> Clean energy buyers, advisors, project developers, utilities and financiers use it to compare power
> purchase agreement (PPA) offers, run NPV and settlement cashflow analysis, evaluate risk scenarios, and
> track projects across North America and Europe. The company also publishes market intelligence
> products including MarketPulse, the LevelTen PPA Price Index, and Market Transparency Reports.
